About Dr. Rushika Ode

I am a resident anaesthesiologist. This website exists because I believe clinical experience only becomes wisdom if it is reflected on, written down, and shared — and because I needed somewhere honest to do that.

Every case is anonymised and published with one purpose: learning. Every pearl is hard-won. Every letter is written with gratitude.

If you're a resident anywhere in the world reading this at midnight before a difficult case — this is for you.
